1. Assess perfusion · Hypernatremia

High sodium alone is not a reason for a bolus. Hypovolemia means reduced circulating volume; dehydration is a water deficit. Address impaired perfusion while planning controlled sodium management; complete dehydration correction is not a prerequisite.

Total-body-water assumption & worked math

Total body water = 0.6 × body weight in kg (liters), retaining the workbook assumption. Neither this estimate nor the sodium-response equation models evolving renal handling or ongoing losses.
